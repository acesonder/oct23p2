About OUTSINC

OUTSINC (Outreach Someone In Need Of Change) is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline and enhance the work of outreach teams, service providers, and administrators working with vulnerable populations.

Our system helps identify specific barriers preventing client stability and provides actionable recommendations linked to available local resources. By centralizing client information and team communication, we enable coordinated care that makes a real difference.
